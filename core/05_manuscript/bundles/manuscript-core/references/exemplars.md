# Style exemplars（摘自作者真实语料，供校准；勿整段照抄到新稿）

## A. English peer review — opening + numbered method points

> This multicenter study develops nomograms combining clinical factors, ADC, and radiomics features from four functional MRI parametric maps (ADC, MSI, PEI, SER) to preoperatively predict DMI, histopathological grade, and LVSI in FIGO stage I endometrial cancer. The clinical question is well-motivated, but the paper's novelty relative to an existing similar multicenter radiomics study (Lefebvre et al., Radiology 2022) is not clearly stated. Methodological transparency is also limited.

> Feature selection was performed on a training set of only 217 patients against 4736 extracted features, with no nested cross-validation or repeated resampling reported to assess selection stability. A single train/external-test split at this feature-to-sample ratio does not fully substitute for stability testing.

> The Conclusion appropriately states that suboptimal intake is "associated with" greater MHE incidence, but the Discussion elsewhere implies causation ("the negligent intake…increases the risk of MHE") and recommends nutritional intervention as "primary prevention." This causal framing is not warranted by an observational design with few events… Conclusions should be consistently framed as hypothesis-generating…

## B. English peer review — stats / internal consistency

> Table 1 shows an internal inconsistency in the adequate group… 28/40 (70.0%) patients are listed with protein intake <0.8 g/Kg/day… directly contradicting the group's defining criterion. The authors must reconcile this discrepancy…

> With only 12 events among 123 participants, the conventional rule of ≥10 events per covariate is violated… The very wide confidence intervals (e.g., HR 10.43; 95% CI 1.27–85.79…) reflect substantial instability that the manuscript understates.

## C. English cover letter (compact contribution)

> Portal-systemic shunting rather than liver dysfunction is the main cause of PSE in chronic hepatic schistosomiasis japonicum (HSJ) patients. Chronic HSJ patients with PSE are frequently misdiagnosed as suffering from dementia, depression, and other psychiatric disorders. However, there are few studies of this type of encephalopathy. In this study, magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) was used as a noninvasive tool… Based on the results, a diagnostic procedure for PSE has also been proposed.

## E. 中文标书摘要节奏（危害→前期→技术/理论创新→拟开展）

> 轻微肝性脑病(MHE)发病率高达84%，不及早干预将进展为肝性脑病，明显增加肝硬化患者死亡率。…前期临床磁共振波谱(1H-MRS)首次发现MHE患者脑乳酸(Lac)升高。但1H-MRS敏感性和特异性低，且无法对Lac进行定量。技术上拟创新性使用13C-MRS结合超极化技术对Lac进行定量。理论上创新性提出Lac通过降低细胞内pH值，上调水通道蛋白4(AQP4)导致星形胶质细胞水肿，最终导致MHE。本项目拟建立MHE大鼠及细胞模型；…期望系统性阐明Lac调控AQP4在MHE中的作用机制。

## F. 中文立题/假说句

> 基于前期研究，申请人提出以下假说：CSH相关MHE的发生源于代谢紊乱与慢性炎症反应共同驱动的脑功能网络重构过程。为验证该假说，本研究将整合代谢物、炎症因子、认知量表、临床特征及功能磁共振（fMRI）等多模态信息，利用跨模态机器学习构建可用于早期预警与病因研究的预测模型。

## G. 中文结题结果段（数字锚）

> 子研究一最终纳入CHS患者127例，其中MHE组42例（33.1%），非MHE组85例。…MHE组门静脉平均流速显著低于非MHE组（12.8±3.5 cm/s vs 16.2±4.1 cm/s, P<0.001）…多变量Logistic回归分析确定门静脉平均流速（OR=0.76, 95%CI: 0.66-0.88, P<0.001）和血小板计数…为MHE的独立预测因子。基于此构建的联合预测模型AUC为0.847（95%CI: 0.774-0.920）…

## H. 中文临床路径论证（解剖→常规路径风险→替代入路）

## I. 中文科普（机制比喻，克制版目标）

> 当纤维化逐渐进展…会导致门静脉高压，就像一条河流被堵塞，水流被迫寻找新的出口。这时，为了缓解高压，身体会自然形成一些新的通道——门体分流。…这些分流允许未经解毒的血液直接进入体循环。这意味着氨和其他有毒物质可以直接进入大脑…
