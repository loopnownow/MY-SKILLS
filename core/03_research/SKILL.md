# Research Design & Evidence

## Purpose

Turn a clinical/biomedical idea into a defensible protocol and evidence base.

**Mounted literature/design/frontier skills are primary for generic retrieval and blueprints** when mounted. Local generic files are a temporary supplement until coverage is confirmed (`EXTERNALIZATION_CANDIDATES.md`). Personal grant/ethics/intake files stay here.

## Literature rule

**Literature research → 03 only.** Do not route literature through 01 (discovery) or 05 (writing). 05 consumes verified I/D evidence; it does not run a second literature-research route.

## Mounted capability ids (generic; PROPOSED until mounted)

- `03-literature` — literature, sources, journals, public datasets
- `03-design` — study design, validation, radiology-design blueprints/endpoints/feasibility
- `03-frontier` — frontier themes, idea-to-question, radiogenomics map

Until mounted, local copies remain: `literature.md`, `sources.md`, `study-design.md`, `radiology-design.md`, `radiology-frontier.md`, etc. (see `EXTERNALIZATION_CANDIDATES.md`).

## Modes

- Evidence: gaps, conflict, mechanism, journal fit. Never invent PMID/DOI.
- Design: question/hypothesis, eligibility, endpoints, sample size, bias, reporting guideline.
- Grant-review (Voice A): load **only** `grant-review.md`. English journal peer review stays in `06_review`.
- Grant-writing (Voice B): skeleton + method-upgrade + `grant-writing.md`. Never paste Voice A tics into own text.
