## Standing letter opener (authoritative in response.md)

After salutation, open with exactly one of:

**Thank you for your comments** | **Thank you for your suggestions** | **Thank you for your inquiry**

- comments = observations / critique
- suggestions = concrete change requests
- inquiry = questions / clarifications

Do not invent a fourth opener noun bank.

## Per-point skeleton (bank-backed)

```text
Thank you for your [comments|suggestions|inquiry].

[1 sentence restating the point]

[Action: We have revised … / We have added … / We clarify that …]

[Pointer: Section / Table / Figure / page-line / Response letter item]
```

| 26 | We thank the reviewer for the comment. / Thank you for your comment. |
| 22 | We appreciate the reviewer's comment. |
| 19 | We thank the reviewer for this comment. |

Prefer variety across points (suggestion vs inquiry vs comment) so the letter does not look template-identical. Avoid nonstandard “reminding”; prefer “pointing this out” / “comment” / “suggestion”.
