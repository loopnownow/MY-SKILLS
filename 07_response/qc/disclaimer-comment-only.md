## Standard (owner 2026-10-21)

For the response letter **正文** and the revised manuscript body under the same package:

- No disclaimer / CYA / incompleteness hedges (e.g. 「某某未做」「我们未做」「未进行…»; *not performed* / *we did not* / *was not done* and kin).
- Such language may appear **only** in Word 批注, and every such 批注 **must** include **建议修改后内容**.
- Do not leave bare flags. Do not invent unrun analyses in the letter.

**PASS:** letter + revised ms body clean; needed incompleteness notes are comments with suggested rewrite.
**FAIL:** strip from body / move to comment with rewrite; mark the matching response point pending if the fact is still undecidable.

Compose with `response-audit.md` / `tighten-qa.md` pointers (bodies stay in fine-ID packs).
