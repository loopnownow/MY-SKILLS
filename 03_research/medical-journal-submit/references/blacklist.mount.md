# Mounted blacklist

This file is mounted on every 选刊 run. Default state is ON.

Never recommend

1. PLoS One (PLOS ONE, Public Library of Science)
2. Scientific Reports (SCI REP-UK, Nature Portfolio)
3. MEDICINE (MEDICINE, Lippincott Williams & Wilkins / Wolters Kluwer, Baltimore general OA journal)

Do not treat New England Journal of Medicine or Nature Medicine as this MEDICINE title.
