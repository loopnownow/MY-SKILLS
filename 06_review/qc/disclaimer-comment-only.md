## Standard (owner 2026-10-21)

When reviewing a manuscript body or a review-report body:

- Scan for disclaimer / CYA / incompleteness hedges (e.g. 「某某未做」「我们未做」「未进行…»; *not performed* / *we did not* / *was not done* and kin).
- Such language **must not** remain in **正文**.
- It may appear **only** in Word 批注 (author **A**).
- Every such 批注 **must** include **建议修改后内容** (suggested revised body wording)—not a bare flag.

**PASS:** no disclaimer/未做-class in body; any needed notes are comments with suggested rewrite.
**FAIL:** body hedge or bare-flag comment → Lee comment (Major if in claimed-final body); Aitee strips body / TC-fix only after owner OK. Do not invent replacement numbers or unrun analyses.
