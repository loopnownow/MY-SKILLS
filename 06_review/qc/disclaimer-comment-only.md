## Standard (owner 2026-10-21; reinforce 2026-10-23 · 否定句)

**Scope — process 否定句 / disclaimer / CYA / incompleteness** (must not stay in 正文):
e.g. 「某某未做」「我们未做」「未进行…»; *not performed* / *we did not* / *was not done* / *not examined* and kin.

**Out of scope — empirical negative findings** that are real study results (e.g. *no significant difference*, *X was not associated with Y*) — those may remain in 正文 when supported by data.

Rules:
- Such process-negation language may appear **only** in Word 批注 (author **A**).
- Every such 批注 **must** include **建议修改后内容** (suggested revised body wording)—not a bare flag.
- Do not invent unrun analyses in body or letter.

**FAIL:** body hedge or bare-flag comment → Lee comment (Major if in claimed-final body); Aitee strips body / TC-fix only after owner OK. Do not invent replacement numbers or unrun analyses.
