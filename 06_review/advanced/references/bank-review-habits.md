## Habit bullets (promote into practice)

1. Check internal consistency (aims ↔ methods ↔ results ↔ claims).
2. Demand explicit inclusion/exclusion and sample-size justification.
3. Separate novelty claim from incremental engineering.
4. Require figure/table callouts to match statistics in text.
5. Flag overclaim when data only support association, not mechanism.
6. In reviews you write: prefer precise hedging over absolute wording; keep locator + Please-fix shape from `review.md`.

## Standing (do not weaken)

- Process 否定句 / CYA → Word 批注 only (author **A**), with 建议修改后内容 — see `review.md` §0 and `05_manuscript/qc/anti-leak-placeholders.md` AL-5.
