## Three hard constraints

1. **Do not invent `.docx` / `.pdf` page layout** unless the owner names it this turn (fonts, margins, line spacing, numbering, headers/footers, figure placement).
2. **Edit text in Track Changes**; prefer **word/sentence-level** edits. **No whole-paragraph silent overwrite** or replace-by-rewrite of a block.
3. **Numbers / stats / inclusion-exclusion wording:** only change wording that does **not** alter outcome / cutoff / covariate / missingness semantics. Do not change those just to make analysis "run".
