## Rule

Before trusting any venue template, page limit, anonymity rule, or required section:

1. Identify exact venue + year/cycle + track + article type + stage.
2. Open the **official** author instructions / CFP / Guide for Authors.
3. Record **URL + date checked** (Asia/Shanghai or local lab date).
4. Distinguish initial submission vs revision vs camera-ready.
5. Treat bundled/generic scaffolds as **not** official unless provenance says so.

Never invent a style-file name by bumping a year in an old filename.
Never present a generic scaffold as an official venue template.

**PASS:** currency note present for the locked venue.
**FAIL:** stale or unsourced template claim → rollback **03 find-journal / 05 write-venue**.
