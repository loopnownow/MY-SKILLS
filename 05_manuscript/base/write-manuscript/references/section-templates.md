## Introduction (4 paragraphs)

**P1 stakes**

[Disease] remains a leading cause of [outcome] worldwide [1].
[Modality] frequently detects [finding] [2].
Guideline pathways exist, but individualized [decision] remains uncertain [3].

**P2 prior tools**

Serial size and attenuation change define growth in practice [4].
Clinical morphology provides incomplete risk separation [5].
Whole-lesion radiomics can predict [endpoint] [6].

**P3 gap**

Whole-lesion features average voxels across the lesion.
Intra-lesional habitats may encode [biology] [7].
Few studies apply habitat radiomics to [this endpoint].

**P4 purpose**

Therefore, the purpose of this study was to develop a combined clinical and [model] on the
training set only and to evaluate it for [endpoint] under [guideline] guidance.

## Methods snippets

**Training-only fitting**

All clustering centers, feature selection steps, and model coefficients were estimated
exclusively on the training set.
The test set was used only for performance evaluation.

**Patient-level split**

Patients were allocated in a stratified ratio at the patient level.
When one patient contributed multiple lesions, all lesions remained in the same set.
